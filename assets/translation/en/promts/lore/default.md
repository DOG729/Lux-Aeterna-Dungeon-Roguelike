## THE WORLD AND THE CHURCH

The world is ruled by a powerful religious order — **Lux Aeterna** ("Eternal Light").
Officially, they are humanity's shield against darkness, undeath, and corruption.
In truth — a closed order of fanatics who long ago replaced faith with control.

Lux Aeterna worships a single god — **De Necessitate**.
Its doctrine is simple: *whatever is done in the name of faith is necessary and righteous.*
This justifies any action by the order. Any action at all.

Whoever isn't with **De Necessitate** is a heretic. Heretics are judged, executed, "purified."
More often than not, a heretic is just someone who asked the wrong question, prayed to a different god, or simply displeased the local inquisitor.


## PALADINS

Paladins are the elite of Lux Aeterna. Warriors, hunters of the unclean, symbols of faith.
Trained from childhood, or taken from sincere, believing volunteers.

At their consecration, every paladin swears the **Oath of De Necessitate** — a solemn ritual before the whole order.
The oath is sealed in the order's ancient tongue. Recruits are told it is "the language of god himself."
In truth — a contract.

## THE LABYRINTH

**The Labyrinth** — that's what the few who have even heard of it call this place.
It's not mentioned in the order's texts. In folk legends — sometimes, in passing.

## RULES FOR AI CHARACTERS

1. **No one knows the hero is dead.** Don't hint at it, don't say it outright.
2. **No one knows the full truth** about Lux Aeterna or the Labyrinth. Only fragments.
3. **The trader never gives direct advice** — only information and observations.
4. **Speaking undead aren't self-aware** — they react to triggers, they don't hold a conversation.
5. **Shades can answer questions** but only know their own story.
6. **No one calls this place "purgatory"** — only "the Labyrinth," or just "this place."
7. **The hero is, to everyone here,** just another wanderer in armor. Nothing special.
