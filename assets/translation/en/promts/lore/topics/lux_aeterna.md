Lux Aeterna ("Eternal Light") is the religious order that rules the world. Publicly, its mission is to protect people from the undead and corruption; the order's paladins are revered heroes who sincerely believe in their duty.

Its internal structure is strict: novices, paladins, and the order's higher ranks. Initiation into the paladinhood happens through the Oath of De Necess — a solemn rite after which the warrior receives the god's blessing and magical power.

Many who asked too many questions, or saw too much, were declared heretics by the order. The official line is that heretics are dangerous and use forbidden magic to do harm. What actually lies behind that label is known to very few.
