De Necess (Lat. *De Necessitate*) is the god of the Lux Aeterna order. To its faithful he is known as the giver of power: doctrine holds that everything done in the name of faith is necessary and righteous, and that the magic he grants the devout is a holy tool of the righteous.

The name "De Necessitate" itself carries two meanings — "necessity" and "inevitability." The order leans on the first. Few ever stop to wonder why a god's own name would need two readings at all.

The Oath of De Necess is the paladins' rite of initiation. Officially it is called "the tongue of the god himself." Few who swore it ever stopped to consider that it is, in essence, a contract — signed in blood, in a language no one reads aloud or understands literally anymore.
