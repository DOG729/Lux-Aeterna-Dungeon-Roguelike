Lux Aeterna — Dungeon Roguelike

Descend into the Labyrinth — a place the Church never speaks of, where oath-breakers and restless souls are said to vanish forever.

You play a fallen soul trapped in endless stone corridors. Fight room by room. Search forgotten chambers. Speak with those who still cling to memory — and those who have already forgotten who they were.

What to expect

• Turn-based battles — strike, guard, heal, flee, or surrender when hope runs out
• Procedural dungeons — new floors, new paths, new dangers each run
• Character growth — skills, stats, and gear that shape how you survive
• Living enemies and strangers — creatures and people with their own voices, temperaments, and stories
• Boss encounters that change as the fight unfolds
• A world narrator that reacts to your victories, defeats, and conversations

The order of Lux Aeterna claims to shield mankind from darkness. In the Labyrinth, that light feels very far away.

Requires an AI language model connection (local or online) so characters can speak. Language support: Russian and English.

Early access / prototype. Content and balance will grow over time.
